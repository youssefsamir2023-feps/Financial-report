Company profile

About the company

Founded in 1997, Obour Land is one of Egypt's leading food manufacturers. Originally focused on white cheese and dairy, it expanded into processed cheese and vegetable fats, and later juices. Listed on the Egyptian Exchange (EGX) in 2016 after years of growth and modernization.

Operations & reach

Exports to 44+ countries. Continuous investment in modern production technology and plant efficiency. Strategic expansion into new product categories supports long-term revenue diversification.

Investment verdict — Conditional Buy
Suitable for growth-oriented investors with moderate-to-high risk tolerance and a 3–5 year horizon. Strong revenue momentum, expanding export footprint, and outstanding ROE make a compelling case. However, the sharp liquidity deterioration and rising leverage demand close monitoring. Conservative investors should adopt a HOLD / WAIT stance until the acid-test ratio recovers and gross margins stabilize. Key triggers to watch: acid-test ratio trend, gross profit margin recovery, and interest coverage staying above 3.5x.
